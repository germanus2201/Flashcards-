Instructions
  • Tap a deck to open it, or the + button on the home screen to create a new one.
  • Tap a deck's ⋮ menu to rename or delete it.
  • Draw with a finger, stylus, or mouse directly on the card.
  • Swipe with two fingers anywhere on the card to flip it over.
  • Drag from the card's edge with one finger to flip it too.
  • Swipe up from the card's edge to cycle to the next card in the deck.
  • Tap ↻ to flip instantly, or + to add a new card above this one.
  • Use undo, redo, and clear to manage strokes on the current side.
  • Turn on palm rejection in Settings to ignore finger touches once a stylus is used.
